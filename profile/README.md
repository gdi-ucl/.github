# Global Disability Innovation Hub

GDI Hub brings research and practice together to advance disability innovation. Based at UCL, its work connects assistive technology, inclusive design and education with global partnerships. Its Academic Research Centre and Community Interest Company contribute complementary research and delivery expertise.

## For researchers

Group published papers and reports into folders in [publications-and-reports](https://github.com/gdi-ucl/publications-and-reports). Create a separate repository for each research or software project. Use your individual GitHub account to collaborate in this shared organisation. Project leads choose public or private visibility and manage their project’s access; only publish material you are authorised to share. Private projects are visible only to their authorised collaborators.

### Join once, then collaborate

1. Send your GitHub username or a verified GitHub email address to the GDI workspace maintainer through the lab’s usual internal channel.
2. Accept the invitation GitHub sends you. Invitations can be issued in batches, but each person accepts their own.
3. Once you are a member, create projects under `gdi-ucl`. To edit a shared repository such as publications-and-reports, ask its maintainer for Write access.

There is no public self-join link. You can browse public resources and copy the public template into your own account without joining the organisation. Do not post colleagues’ email lists in public Issues.
